# Sponsor Transparency

> Canonical editable version lives in Notion. This file is the version-controlled mirror.

The operations guide references a shared transparency doc for tiered cuts. This is that doc. Until it existed, the payment model had no operational home.

## Why this exists

Sponsorships subsidize accessibility — they are what let a workshop stay $10–50 instead of $75. Everyone should be able to see where the money went without having to ask.

## What we already agreed

- **Popup shoots:** Sunshine and Bingle split revenue 50/50, project-based. Each shoot is its own event.
- **Sponsorships:** tiered cuts above a threshold. Whoever closes the sponsor receives a larger percentage. Sunshine takes a founder cut. The business retains a portion for operations and growth.
- **Payment timing:** everyone is paid within 7 days of sponsor funds *clearing* — not when the sponsor commits, when the money actually arrives.

## Revenue context

- **Open Studio + Drop-ins:** $0–400 per month ($10 entry, sponsor-supported)
- **Workshop:** $150–750 per month ($10–50 per person, ~15 people)
- **Sponsorships:** $300–1.5k, scaling with brand and tier
- **Geyser events:** add $1,500–5,000+ depending on sponsors and ticket volume

Ticket sales are never the only revenue line.

## The rule this protects

Sponsorship outreach currently sits with Sunshine (single owner) and Monny (open invitation). Revenue that depends on an optional role is fragile. A target list plus a pitch template makes outreach available to anyone with capacity — which is what makes the money line resilient.
